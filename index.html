OUR FOUNDER

Alpesh Vaishnav

Founder & Trustee

Shreeji Blood Bank was started with a vision of helping people receive blood when they need it most.

Founder Alpesh Vaishnav was inspired by his father's experience with thalassemia. This personal experience became a motivation to create a service that could help patients and families during difficult times.

His goal is to inspire more people to become voluntary blood donors and create a stronger culture of blood donation in society.

"One small act of kindness can become someone's greatest hope."
